Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your personal iCloud account. Keepit has no servers and we can never see your receipts or tasks. See our privacy policy.

I got a new phone — how do I get my data back?

Install Keepit and sign in to the same Apple Account (iCloud). Your receipts and tasks sync back automatically within a few minutes.

I bought Keepit Pro but it shows as locked.

Open Keepit → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Reminders aren't showing up.

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Keepit and make sure notifications are allowed. Keepit sends reminders at 10 AM local time before tasks are due, return windows close, and warranties expire.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete your items in the app, or remove Keepit's iCloud data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. We hold no copies.
